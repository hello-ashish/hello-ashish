## ⚡ About Me

I am **Ashish Gupta**, a developer and the founder of **AroundUs**. My public work ranges from browser-based productivity tools and real-time web experiments to AI-powered applications, security-focused platforms, mobile software, and systems projects. 

I enjoy turning a rough idea into something people can use: defining the problem, shaping the experience, and building the product all the way through.

- 🔭 **Current focus:** Building AroundUs and developing secure web platforms
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Advanced WebRTC and AI integrations
- 💬 **Ask me about:** JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Java, Kotlin, C++, React, WebRTC
- 📫 **How to reach me:** agupta118258@gmail.com
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I can turn a rough idea into a working prototype in a single weekend!
